Leagues in play

Pick at least one. A league that is switched off is ignored when the slate is built, and its target counts for nothing.

The size and the games can be changed at any time until the first player submits a pick for that week. After that the count is fixed and only voiding a game is available, so scoring stays consistent.

Pinned rivalry games

One matchup per line, "Team A vs Team B", college teams only. The first time either side of one of these matchups shows up on the schedule, the game is pinned onto the slate automatically, no matter how wide the spread runs, since closest-spread alone would routinely leave out a game like Ohio State vs Michigan the moment either team is having a blowout season. Pinning is a proposal, not a lock: the commissioner can still unpin any single game from the slate editor without losing this list, and that choice sticks across later rebuilds of the same game.

Scoring

How points run

A wrong pick counts its staked confidence against the player. A correct pick earns nothing. Lowest total for the week wins. A player who submits no picks takes the maximum possible penalty for that week, so sitting a week out is never the safe play.

A correct pick earns its staked confidence. A wrong pick earns nothing. Highest total for the week wins. A player who submits no picks scores 0 for that week.

This pool's real rule is inverse. Standard is here for a pool that wants the older, familiar scoring instead. Changing this only affects weeks scored after you save it, weeks already scored keep the points they were scored with until you rescore them.
