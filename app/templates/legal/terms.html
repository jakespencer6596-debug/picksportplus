Pools and commissioners

Each pool has a commissioner who sets it up and runs it. The commissioner controls the league name, the join code, the season, the timezone, the lock time, how many games are on the slate each week, and how many of those come from each league. They can also edit the proposed slate, void a game that is cancelled or moved, and remove members.

Removing a member deletes that member's picks and results for the pool. Commissioner decisions about their own pool are final as far as the app is concerned. We do not arbitrate disputes between members of a pool.

Picks and scoring

  • Picks for a week must be submitted before that week's lock time, which is normally the first kickoff on the slate. The lock is enforced by the clock.
  • After the lock, picks become visible to everyone in your pool. That is deliberate, so the pool can see the week is honest.
  • If you do not submit before the lock, you score zero for that week.
  • A tie, a cancellation, or a game the commissioner voids scores nothing for anyone and is left out of the correct and possible counts.
  • Results are recalculated as scores come in and may change until a week is final.
